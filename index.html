typepper — a spicy little typing game

A typing game and typing test where your speed is heat: type fast and clean to climb the Scoville scale from bell pepper to Carolina reaper, with combos, streaks and fever. Modes: sprint (10, 25 or 50 words), blitz (15, 30 or 60 seconds), zen (endless, no clock), survival (a fuel clock that drains faster every level), floor is lava (words fall toward rising lava; type them before they land) and hot pursuit (a cop chases your caret down the text you've typed; a wrong key stops you cold) and the hot one (three slips and a plate of peppers comes round; bite the hot one and you're done; play it alone or in a room with friends, last one standing wins). Survival, floor is lava and hot pursuit start from the level you pick. Personal bests are saved.

Play it here in your browser, or install it in your terminal on macOS, Linux and Windows. Free and open source under the GPL.
